How to use Tiger Timer

Power & charging

  • The battery only charges while the power switch is ON: switch it on, then plug in USB-C. Leaving it on is fine, the box goes to sleep on its own.
  • While it charges, the light blinks red: that's how you know it's charging.
  • Switch it off for storage: that fully disconnects the battery.

Controls

  • Arcade button: confirm, or the game's main action.
  • Turn the knob: move through menus and change values.
  • Click the knob: confirm in menus; pause/resume in Timer and Chess Clock.
  • Hold the knob and turn: volume, from anywhere.
  • Hold the knob 1 s inside a game: “Go back to menu?”. It's the only way out of a game, so a round is never lost by accident.
  • Hold the arcade button while the box starts: skip the intro animation.

Sleep

  • The screen turns off after 5 minutes without use (not while a timer or game is running). Any button or knob turn wakes it; that first press only wakes it.
  • After 30 minutes the box goes into deep sleep. Press the arcade button to start it again.

Settings on the box

  • Language (English, Français, Español), Timer Step (how fast the Timer length changes per knob click), and Remote Config (this page).
  • Press either button on the box to leave Remote Config. It also closes on its own after 5 minutes with nobody connected.
